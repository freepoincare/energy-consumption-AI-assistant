  ### 2. How Each Metric is Calculated

  1. Overall Statistics:
      • total_consumption_kwh: ∑daily_consumption_kwh = 941.42  kWh.
      • average_daily_consumption_kwh: Total/184  days = 5.12  kWh/day.
      • median_daily_consumption_kwh & std_daily_consumption_kwh: 5.14 kWh/day and 2.08 kWh/day.
  2. Monthly Aggregation:
      • Grouping records by YYYY-MM. Calculates total kWh and daily average for each month (e.g., March: 232.63 kWh / 7.50 kWh/day; August: 112.71 kWh / 3.64 kWh/day).
  3. Weekday vs Weekend:
      • Compares Monday–Friday days (5.11 kWh/day) against Saturday–Sunday (5.13 kWh/day), showing virtually uniform baseline usage (+0.4%).
  4. Day-of-Week Patterns:
      • Grouping across all Mondays, Tuesdays, etc. Monday is highest (5.39 kWh/day), Thursday is lowest (4.62 kWh/day).
  5. Recent Periods:
      • Rolling slices of the most recent 7 and 30 days compared against the overall 6-month average (-24.6% and -30.3% respectively).
  6. Deterministic Trend:
      • Short-Term: 7-day rolling window comparison (recent 7 days vs previous 7 days) showing +21.3% short-term increase.
      • Overall / Long-Term: Linear regression slope (-0.0231  kWh/day) and boundary comparison showing a -52.5% reduction across the spring-to-summer transition.
  7. Cost Aggregation:
      • Sums estimated rate charges (£245.24 total / £1.33 per day) with explicit disclaimers.

  ──────
  ### 3. Why Specific Sections are Included

  • Max / Min Dates: Enables the AI assistant to directly answer questions like "When did I use the most electricity?" (March 16, 2026: 12.21 kWh) without querying
  individual raw timestamps.
  • Monthly & Day-of-Week Breakdown: Answers high-level behavioral questions ("Which month was most expensive?", "Do I consume more on weekends?").
  • Recent Periods & Trend: Allows the AI to provide immediate context on whether recent consumption is rising or falling relative to historical habits.
  ──────
  ### 4. Summary Boundaries & Direct Database Queries

  • Summary Boundary: The summary provides global and sectional aggregations to keep prompt context concise (~500 tokens).
  • Why Specific Dates Require Direct Queries: Answering questions such as "How much did I use on June 14, 2026?" or "What was my usage during the week of July 10?" is
  not in the summary by design. In future tool/function calling steps, the AI will issue targeted Firestore queries (GET /api/data/{date}) for exact date ranges.

  ### 5. How STEP 6 Will Consume This Summary
