  ## 7. Remaining Limitations

  1. Context-Only Retrieval for Specific Dates: Because only aggregated summary metrics and extreme dates are injected into the system prompt, individual dates (e.g.
  2026-07-15) cannot be answered without implementing OpenAI Function Calling or database tool lookups.
  2. Cold Starts on Free Tier: Hosting on Render's free tier can cause a 30–50 second cold-start delay on the initial request when the service spins down after
  inactivity.
  3. Estimated Cost Scope: Cost figures reflect simple multiplication against static unit rates and do not incorporate complex tiered utility billing, time-of-use
  tariffs, standing daily fees, or VAT.
